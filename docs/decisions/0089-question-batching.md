## Question
How are the grilling program's questions asked?

## Facts
- 0020 and plan section 4: one question per decision; related questions share a call, up to the tool's limit (four per call in the session of 2026-10-07).
- The program covers roughly 300 decisions, about 80 calls at four per call.

## Options
| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Grouped confirmations | A slice's low-stakes decisions whose recommendation carries over unchanged come as one tick-list, and anything he unticks gets its own question; about half the calls | A ticked item gets less discussion |
| One per decision | Full facts, options and table for every decision | About 80 calls |

## Recommendation
Grouped confirmations; consequential, new or changed decisions always get their own question.

## Choice
Grouped confirmations.

## Reason
Not given.

## Consequences
- A call holds up to the tool's limit of questions. One may be a tick-list of a slice's low-stakes decisions: the recommendation is the plan's first option, no fact changed, and it has no cost, safety or privacy effect. Each item shows its one-line facts and the recommended option; each unticked item comes back as its own full question.
- Plan section 4 and AGENTS.md's "No drift" rules say so.
